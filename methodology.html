Traveler-first review model

The report prioritizes journey tasks over technical-only checks. It combines traveler tasks, technical heuristics, and manual verification prompts.

Evidence rules

No operational claims are invented. If evidence cannot be verified from fixture/manual input, it is marked as “Needs manual verification.”

Legal note

Automated checks cannot guarantee legal compliance. Human screen-reader and keyboard testing are required for WCAG 2.2 AA, BITV 2.0, BFSG, and EN 301 549-aligned outcomes.
